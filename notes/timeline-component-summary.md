The forecast timeline sits at the bottom of the main screen, beneath the weather readout and precipitation and wind indicators. It arranges forecast days horizontally, with each day label centered over its position, a condition icon below, a thin rail with day boundary ticks, and the daily high beneath the rail; the selected day’s label and high are emphasized. A gold playhead stays fixed at the center as the timeline moves beneath it, and a badge below shows the selected local time. Dragging left scrubs forward and updates the main weather scene and forecast values; an ADB swipe moved the selection from Sunday morning to Wednesday at about 7:22 PM CST, where the screen showed 60°F and cloudy or overcast conditions, a 40% precipitation chance, 5 mph wind, and a 67°/53° high and low. Double-tapping a day enters hourly precision near 7 a.m.; hourly ticks replace the daily icons and highs while the day labels and playhead remain, and forecast browsing plus an accessibility action provide alternate ways to browse values or switch scales.
