## Contract review decisions

- Treat the current `CanonicalForecastProjection.sceneTransition()` blend as decorative continuity between source samples. `ExpectedFieldSupport.Points` establishes expected sample instants and `CoverageStatus.COMPLETE` confirms supplied values at those points. Two adjacent supported samples with different conditions can bracket a change between their valid times, but the blend fraction does not report its exact onset or clearing minute, duration, or continuous condition coverage. Explicit accumulation intervals support amounts over their bounds, not continuous rain or exact onset/clearing. Review the SG draft against these contracts and product granularity: an empty condition set alone cannot establish dry weather, probability alone cannot establish an event or intensity, and unavailable coverage cannot inherit a convincing prior condition.
- Document the owner-approved between-sample rule in plain terms: imagery may represent and blend adjacent supported condition samples, and a change between them may be communicated as occurring within their time bracket. Keep source valid times and granularity in readable facts; do not describe the visual transition point as a source-reported event time or measured minute-by-minute weather. An expected missing sample or unsupported gap breaks that inference and visual carry. SG.1 must define the bounded assignment/display policy for between-sample selection, including gap behavior and disclosure; SG.4 must decide which precipitation inputs establish supported dry/event intervals and how point-supported rain/snow differs from interval-backed amount/rate. Until those later contracts are approved, SG.0 must not assign exact event start/end times to point conditions.
- Preserve the existing selected-time path: `CatalogTimelineView.selectedInstant` is supplied to `CatalogTimeLabScreen`, which owns the timeline interaction. A scene evaluates that instant. Loading, theme changes, motion, and scale changes do not create or retarget another selected time. Retained imagery during loading must not appear to describe facts at the new selection.
- Keep scene planning and theme composition separate in the reviewed contract. A plan describes supported semantic content and time without placement or Compose drawable objects. Theme composition chooses assets, parameters, background, placement, and order; the renderer draws the result. Correct the SG draft's planning/cache wording where it makes theme version a semantic-plan input: forecast inputs/window and generator rules determine the plan; theme version and relevant viewport inputs determine composition; selected instant determines evaluation. Do not prescribe a cache implementation or new Gradle module in this review.
- Use the approved board's dry → rain → unavailable → dry sequence, the independent rain/light-phase sequence, and same-plan/two-theme comparison as concrete review examples. State any unresolved mapping or owner choice at the later slice where it must be settled. Prefer concise normative rules and examples over additional speculative types, registries, or generic framework language.
- Use `docs/WEATHER_ASSETS_SPEC.md` as the candidate semantic inventory, not a demand for one drawing per row or immediate worldwide coverage. Compare its forecast IDs with `WeatherCondition`, `WeatherPhenomenon`, and other supported canonical fields; distinguish currently representable meanings, shared artwork with distinct text, future data/asset families, and unknown fallback. Preserve regional meanings such as sleet and the separation between forecast conditions, hazards, and official alerts. Do not silently add catalog enums, provider mappings, assets, or a second forecast model in SG.0.
