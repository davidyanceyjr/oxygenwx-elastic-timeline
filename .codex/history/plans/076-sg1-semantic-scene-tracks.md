## Functional invariants

- The selected `Instant` remains the sole scene reading time. Plan generation is deterministic from catalog identity/revision, timezone, absolute requested window, and generator version; theme, viewport, and selected instant do not change semantic-plan identity. Evaluation never moves selected time or changes source facts.
- Preserve `ExpectedFieldSupport.Points` as points and `ExpectedFieldSupport.Intervals` as source-backed half-open intervals. Sort by `Instant` across DST. Neither complete day coverage nor adjacent supplied points becomes a continuous weather interval.
- Each supplied point keeps its source valid time and may represent only its bounded nearest expected-time cell where the hourly adjacency rule below establishes one. Define deterministic midpoint tie handling. A missing expected slot, unsupported gap, or first/last boundary breaks visual carry. Keep source time, support kind, and representative display cell separately inspectable. A display boundary is never a reported event minute.
- **Point-cell contract:** For each point-supported field, order its expected `Instant`s and attach a supplied value only to its matching expected slot. For the current hourly products, construct nonzero representative cells only across consecutive expected slots exactly one elapsed hour apart within the same local date and, where present, the same source/product segment. Assign selection to the nearest expected instant; at an exact midpoint, the earlier slot owns the tie, including when that slot is missing. Express boundary ownership explicitly even if ranges are stored as half-open. Derive an outer edge only from an adjacent supported hourly spacing and clip it to the requested window, source/product boundary, and local-day bounds. An isolated slot or a wider/sparse pair without declared cadence has no inferred span: its supplied value is available at its exact source instant, and other times in the unproven gap evaluate unknown. Do not infer a general cadence from the spacing of sparse points or join cells across an unsupported source/product gap. A missing expected value owns an unknown cell and interrupts both hold and blend. These are display cells, never source validity intervals or inferred event times.
- **Evidence separation:** The plan and evaluation expose the expected slot, supplied source valid time (when present), support kind, representative cell, and exact selected `Instant` as distinct values. Evaluate weather and light independently. A probability-only slot stays probability-only; an interval amount retains its original half-open bounds and cannot fill an unknown condition cell. Keep the existing `CanonicalForecastProjection.sceneTransition()` behavior unchanged in SG.1; it is not a source-aware input to this generator.
- Distinguish explicitly supported sky/weather conditions, unknown/missing, probability-only, and interval-backed amounts/rates. `CLEAR` describes sky/weather appearance, not a general no-precipitation assertion. An empty condition set, zero probability, or zero amount alone does not establish a categorical dry forecast. An amount/rate interval does not by itself claim continuous rain or snow. SG.1 preserves the supplied signals without introducing `DRY` into `WeatherCondition` or deriving a daily dry label; SG.4 owns precipitation-specific dry/event evidence, intensity, and qualified daily wording such as “mostly dry.”
- A versioned local-time day/dusk/night rule supplies visual variants without claiming astronomical sunrise/sunset or moon phase. Light tracks do not change weather support. Preserve timeline responsiveness, gesture ownership, readable forecast facts, honest provenance/DEMO DATA, accessibility, RTL, and reduced-motion meaning. No UI behavior changes in SG.1.

## Risks and assumptions

- Current `CanonicalForecastProjection` chooses a nearest fact and same-day decorative blend without expected-slot gap awareness; using it directly could misrepresent missing support. The generator must consult expected support through the narrow `CatalogDay` accessor specified above.
- Expected support lists instants but does not declare cadence across sparse slots. SG.1 uses the current hourly adjacency rule above and leaves wider unproven gaps unknown; a future nonhourly product needs its own explicit support policy before acquiring representative spans. This does not justify expanding the catalog schema or claiming continuous coverage.
- The canonical vocabulary is narrower than the proposed weather-assets inventory and has no general `DRY` condition. Operational forecasts use “dry” in period outlook language, often alongside a sky condition and a separate precipitation chance; this does not supply an SG.1 point-condition mapping. Unsupported meanings remain unknown or later work; SG.4 determines precipitation-specific dry/event and intensity mappings and any daily dry wording.
