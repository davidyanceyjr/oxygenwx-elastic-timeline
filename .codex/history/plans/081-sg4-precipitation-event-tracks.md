- A supplied `conditions` point in its SG.1 representative cell selects `RAIN` or `SNOW` when exactly one of those supported event types is present. A nonempty supplied set containing only `CLEAR`, `PARTLY_CLOUDY`, or `CLOUDY` selects no falling precipitation while retaining its exact supplied readable condition, regardless of separately supplied probability or amount. An accepted absent expected point uses the preceding supplied hourly condition only under the amended carry contract, retaining that point's original source time. A leading gap has no carried hourly condition. Keep the existing storm depiction outside this precipitation choice; unsupported or mixed precipitation categories do not acquire a guessed SG.4 type.
- Where no supported categorical value selects rain, snow, or a nonprecipitation condition, a separately supplied **positive** `rain_amount_millimeters` or `snowfall_centimeters` interval may select its named art only while the selected instant is inside that interval's original half-open bounds. An explicit zero remains a displayed supplied fact but creates no falling-precipitation art and does not itself establish a clear/cloud condition. If both typed interval families support conflicting types without a supported categorical choice, do not invent a mixed scene or display a placeholder condition label. Snow depth, generic total precipitation, and untyped precipitation rate cannot choose rain or snow. A rate's source mean interval is retained for its readable fact, never promoted into a point condition cell.
- An accepted absent condition point follows the amended preceding-hour carry rule; its displayed condition is explicitly sourced from that preceding point. A separately supported typed interval keeps its own bounds and is not erased by the absent point; it cannot fill the missing hourly source fact. Probability alone, including zero probability, remains chance text and never selects art, a clear/cloud condition, particle count, or intensity. Do not use meteorological plausibility checks to reject supplied supported categories.
- Keep source valid times and supplied interval bounds in readable facts. The selected timestamp drives the scene; representative-cell boundaries are illustrative only. On adjacent hourly expected slots on the same local date, SG.1 assigns the exact midpoint tie to the **earlier** slot, including a missing slot eligible for carry. For example, supported `CLEAR` at 19:00 and `RAIN` at 20:00 show the clear representative scene at exactly 19:30, then the rain representative scene immediately after 19:30 and at 20:00, without claiming an exact rain onset. A daily clear description does not override a supported selected-time rain condition. Solar phase and event geometry continue to use the selected date's provider-backed events, whatever the precipitation choice.
- Keep the first-theme precipitation field deterministic and illustrative. Normal is the baseline for a supported rain/snow choice without explicit source intensity. Light and Heavy variants are visual definitions only in SG.4; live selection requires the later optional source-intensity data slice and its approved mapping. Do not infer intensity from probability or arbitrary amount/rate thresholds, or imply measured particle density. Draw precipitation below its source clouds and behind forecast/timeline text. Rain may obscure or strongly subdue the direct solar cue without changing its source event times, selected-time light phase, or supplied cloud-cover percentage. The existing non-catalog scene behavior and storm visuals remain unchanged.
