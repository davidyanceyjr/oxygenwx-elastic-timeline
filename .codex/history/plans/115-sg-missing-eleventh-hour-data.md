## Approved scene-specification amendment

As the first execution step, amend the normative SG.1 rule in `docs/OXYGEN_SCENE_GENERATION_SPECIFICATION.md` to state that a directly supplied value at a date's final expected hourly point may use its otherwise-eligible SG.1 representative cell through that local date's exclusive midnight boundary. Identify the final slot in absolute-time order within the IANA local date, and clip the cell to the requested window. The selected `Instant` and the point's source valid time remain separate; disclose the original source time. This is a same-date display association, not a new observation, interval, or claim of continuous weather.

The amendment must also state the limits already approved by the behavior board: apply eligibility independently to each point field; a missing, empty, or unsupported final field stays unavailable and does not extend the previous point through a trailing gap; isolated and wider/sparse points retain SG.1's no-span behavior; the next local date takes ownership at midnight; accumulation and rate intervals keep their existing bounds. Precipitation chance remains a probability signal and never supplies a condition claim. Temperature and wind text use their own supported point values even when condition is unavailable, with each field's source time preserved.

Reconcile SG.4's text and examples to make clear that its bracketed, same-date interior-condition carry is unchanged, does not extend through trailing missing condition slots, and never crosses local midnight. Rule 6 of `docs/SPECIFICATION.md` says a trailing gap makes no hourly condition claim; add a narrow clarification that a directly supplied final point displayed in its SG.1-eligible same-date cell through exclusive midnight is not a missing trailing slot or a carry. Keep its source valid time disclosed and midnight owned by the next local date. Do not relax the missing-source, no-cross-date, or no-continuous-coverage invariants.

## Functional invariants

- A source point retains its exact valid time. A representative display cell is not a measured minute-level observation or an event-onset time. The selected `Instant` and playhead remain unchanged through scene evaluation, date rollover, and scale animation.
- Supplied zero, missing, empty, unsupported, isolated, and sparse values keep distinct meanings. Probability, amount, rate, condition, temperature, and wind are independent signals. A missing condition does not become a dry or clear condition claim.
- A directly supported final point may represent only its own local date under the approved rule. The next date owns midnight; do not carry a fact across the date boundary or change another date's high/low.
- Preserve source/provenance truth, readable text and accessible meaning, timeline responsiveness, gesture ownership, city scene layering, and SG.4's separate eligible interior missing-condition rule.
