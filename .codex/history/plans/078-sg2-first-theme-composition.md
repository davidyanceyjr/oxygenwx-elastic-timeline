## Functional invariants

- `SemanticScenePlan` and forecast presentation remain theme-independent. The presenter constructs the plan once per active catalog generation and evaluates it at the exact selected instant of the readable projection; theme composition consumes that paired `SceneReading`, including its SG.1 light phase, without changing event support/timing, facts, provenance, missingness, or selection. Theme and viewport affect placement/asset selection only. There is no second time state, redraw-time plan generation, or theme-derived forecast fact.
- Layer order and placement belong to this city theme and are deterministic for a fixed theme version, scene input, and viewport. Do not assume every theme has a sky, ground, horizon, or the same foreground/background roles.
- City background variants compose continuously or deterministically across the SG.1 day/dusk/night phases; changing light variants cannot move or rewrite weather tracks. Morning and noon are theme visual treatments of SG.1 `DAY`, selected from the paired selected instant and catalog timezone, not additional semantic phases. Daytime building and street lights remain off. The sun/moon marker is a static decorative local-time cue at that instant, with no sunrise, sunset, position, or phase assertion. Existing weather-function shading may continue using its current `SceneVisualState` inputs; this slice does not remap those weather functions to SG.1 tracks.
- Auxiliary city details remain semantically non-weather even when interleaved at theme-selected depths. Their static presentation communicates the same selected-time scene meaning when animation is reduced or disabled.
- Assets preserve aspect/composition within the declared scene world and fit the approved portrait phone and compact viewports without covering important forecast facts or timeline labels. Scene decoration adds no text requiring font scaling or RTL mirroring; record those applicability decisions and check the relevant inherited UI behavior.
- Keep the existing timeline responsive, readable, and gesture ownership unchanged. Do not add persistent controls or accessibility semantics that compete with forecast facts.
