# OX-33 Settings entry and catalog list artwork supplement

**Status:** Product-owner approved on 2026-11-23. The SVG is the
editable visual source and the paired PNG is its review render.

## States shown

- Existing forecast screen with a visible gear next to the World control. The
  gear has the accessible name “Settings”; forecast facts and timeline remain
  present and unchanged.
- Read-only Settings screen with a back action and a “Weather world” list.
- Living Urban Park appears as the bundled-free role and may be marked Active
  when it is the committed world. It has no content revision until one exists;
  the UI does not invent or display a version.
- App-owned rows show their distinct Compatible, Incompatible, or Disabled
  catalog status as text. Unavailable states are not conveyed by color alone.
- The rows do not select a theme. OX-69 owns that behavior.

This supplement adds only the Settings entry point and makes the bundled-role
revision boundary visible. It does not alter the approved scene, forecast
values, timeline, or OX-33 read-only list scope.
