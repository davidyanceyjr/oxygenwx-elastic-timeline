# OX-175 pack platform readiness UI artwork contract

**Status:** Proposed for product-owner review. This conceptual board does not
claim installed acceptance or authorize implementation.

Source artwork: `.codex/concepts/OX-175-pack-platform-readiness-ui-artwork-board.svg`.
The paired PNG is the review render. Both files serve distinct roles: editable
vector source and fixed-size raster review copy.

## Affected states

- **OX-33:** A simple Settings theme list contains the OX-41 bundled free
  Living Urban Park, installed compatible entries, active state, and a clear
  disabled/incompatible entry. Status remains readable as text. It is backed
  by the app-managed catalog and adds no shared-storage scan.
- **OX-69:** Selecting a compatible entry indicates the active row and follows
  the existing OX-32 switch path. The artwork adds no chooser or loading
  mechanism of its own and shows no download or checkout state.
- **OX-61:** A still-compatible saved pack/variant returns as the visible world
  after cold launch. The same selected instant and forecast facts remain
  visible and the timeline selection remains in place.
- **OX-62:** If the saved choice is unavailable, the visible world returns to
  the OX-41 bundled free default, Living Urban Park. The catalog continues to
  identify the saved entry as unavailable. OX-56's First City fallback is a
  different, already-approved scene-resolution case. The board adds no
  banner, dialog, or cause text and does not require Park polish.
- **OX-09:** Paired samples use the same world, selected instant, and supplied
  inputs. Their scene output should match; a stable-seed correction is in
  scope only if the behavior evidence demonstrates re-randomization. The
  artwork is illustrative and records no observed result.
- **OX-39:** One existing renderer capability can show two declared treatments
  with the same weather meaning, element identity, and depth. First City
  CANOPY is the evidence-backed candidate from OX-24; the exact alternate
  treatment remains subject to behavior-board review.
- **OX-40:** The review compares First City CANOPY with the adapted left
  Open Landscape tree group from OX-92. It shows the same shared weather
  meaning without making the two worlds' artwork identical or requiring Park.
- **OX-59:** A single scrub/switch overlap ends with the new active scene and
  the same exact selected instant. It introduces no gesture, control, or
  permanent chrome.
- **OX-60:** After a theme replacement, only the new theme's layers, hit
  targets, and animation state remain current. The schematic ghost denotes
  resources scheduled for retirement; it is not a requested visible effect.

## Visual constraints

- Keep the immersive weather scene and timeline primary. Forecast facts remain
  visible as text and retain their supplied, carried, or unavailable meaning.
- Keep the Settings rows direct and low-chrome; active, compatible, and
  unavailable states are distinguishable without color alone.
- Use schematic scenes only to communicate affected states. The artwork does
  not specify new weather values, a new world composition, Park mural/art
  polish, or a production launch screen.
- This board covers UI artwork only for OX-09, OX-33, OX-39, OX-40, OX-59,
  OX-60, OX-61, OX-62, and OX-69. The remaining roster slices keep behavior
  boards but have no visible-output change in their current roadmap scope.

## Approval and evidence boundary

Owner approval applies only to the exact SVG and PNG files reviewed together
with the cycle 175 behavior package. Any edit requires refreshed renders and
another owner review. These boards guide scope and acceptance; they do not
replace installed screenshots and scoped interactions for slices whose exits
require on-device visual evidence. Cycle 175 has no installed evidence yet.
